European Era is an intermediary host organisation with decades of vocational education experience. We work with colleges, universities, and agencies across Europe to host VET students and staff, graduates, and professionals under Erasmus+ KA1 and related programmes — including placement, accommodation, language training, and cultural activities from our hubs in Cork and Málaga.
