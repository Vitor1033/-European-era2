About European Era

We are an intermediary host company specialised in international mobility — with over 30 years of vocational education experience and teams in Cork (Ireland) and Málaga (Spain). We host VET students and staff, graduates, and professionals who want practical experience, new perspectives, and the benefits of Erasmus+ and related programmes.
