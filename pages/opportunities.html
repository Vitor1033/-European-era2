Who can apply

Opportunities are published for learners and graduates eligible under their sending institution’s Erasmus+ or mobility agreement. Final selection always respects your home university’s rules and the host organisation’s requirements.

  • Valid learning agreement or institutional nomination where required
  • Professional English for most placements (B2+ recommended)
  • Willingness to complete pre-mobility tasks on time
  • Health and travel insurance as defined by your programme
  • Respect for host workplace codes and local regulations

Before you apply

Have these items ready so your home institution can approve quickly and we can move you to matching without delays.

  1. Updated CV focused on outcomes and tools used
  2. Motivation letter (role + destination + dates)
  3. Language self-assessment or certificates if available
  4. Preferred start window and any academic constraints
  5. Emergency contact details for mobility insurance forms
